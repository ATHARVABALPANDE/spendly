Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Spendly personal expense tracking application and website (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and a hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter to track your spending.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type, device, and pages visited, used to keep the Service working and secure.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core features of Spendly — storing your expenses and generating summaries and category breakdowns.
  • To secure your account and detect or prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the Service based on aggregated, non-identifying usage trends.

3. What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not share your expense records with advertisers. We do not use your data to make lending, credit, or eligibility decisions.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a database protected by access controls. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text. While no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and export the expense data associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate information by editing your entries or profile.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time.

7. Cookies

Spendly uses a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in and maintain your session. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the Service.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at support@spendly.example.
